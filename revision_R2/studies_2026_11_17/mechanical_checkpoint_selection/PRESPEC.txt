SAVED-CHECKPOINT SELECTION STUDY — 17 NOVEMBER 2026
Written before outer predictions or scores for any last checkpoint are obtained.

Reason and scope
Previous stopping-only diagnostics showed that later saved checkpoints improve
mechanical predictions while often worsening direct maxima. The existing
maximum-selected graph and no-message controls are already known. This secondary
study asks whether choosing between the two SAVED graph checkpoints by joint
stopping loss improves mechanical predictions on the unchanged outer cohorts.
It is not neural retraining, a new holdout, a full joint-loss training trajectory,
or a search over epochs. Both saved checkpoints arose from maximum-based
scheduling and patience. The selection rule is motivated by observed stopping
results and historical training provenance; it was not chosen before those data.

Fixed selection rule
For each of the exact ten existing partitions, use the 75 stopping SVEs only.
Compute L = 0.5 node loss + 0.5 graph loss with the original executed loss recipe,
original transformed targets and missing-label convention, batch size 16 and
original ordered stopping IDs. Average batch losses weighted by number of SVEs.
Choose last.pt only if its L is strictly less than best.pt's L; ties choose best.
Reuse the independently verified stopping-only caches and recorded batch losses.
Recompute their aggregation and freeze all ten choices in selection.json before
any outer inference. Both checkpoints remain eligible in every fold. There is
one encoder choice for ALL quantities, never a different choice by case or target.
This uses the same joint loss family as the original model; it need not reproduce
historical batching/reduction, and does not reproduce historical scheduling.

Endpoints and fixed comparisons
Evaluate both saved checkpoints on all ten ordered outer cohorts, then assemble:
  original_maximum_selected: the unchanged verified published-reference mechanics;
  best_replay: CPU replay for implementation parity only;
  last_fixed: fixed final-checkpoint diagnostic, not an outcome-selected winner;
  joint_selected: the stopping-only selection above, main candidate of this study.
Six mechanical quantities, their exact existing physical truths and MPa units;
all 940 SVEs, two five-fold repeats, each size and pooled. Compare joint_selected
and last_fixed with the original, RF/HGB under fitting-only and fitting+stopping
budgets, and corresponding size means from the verified mechanical-tree study.
Do not reinterpret joint-selected versus old no-message controls as a matched
architecture ablation: those controls have no saved last checkpoint.

Preserve full transformed node/graph predictions and raw means for both stopping
and outer cohorts, including raw node truth/masks, grain identifiers, offsets,
four grain peaks, direct maxima, six mechanics, all auxiliary output channels.
Reuse stopping NPZs by immutable path plus hash; newly write outer NPZs here.
No fitting set or outer label is used to select the checkpoint.

Assess the SAME selected encoder's four direct maximum predictions and existing
calibrated selected-grain bridge. For each best/last checkpoint independently,
fit the existing fixed StandardScaler + Ridge(alpha=10) scalar bridge map on its
eligible stopping predictions only. This alpha matches the existing executed
bridge implementation (including its method-name dispatch), not assumed OLS.
The selected-grain scalar and eligibility are exactly the established summaries:
mean of selected peaks + sqrt(6)/pi * SD(selected peaks) * log(effective count),
at least four selected and positive SD; fitting-size mean fallback for ineligible
outer cases, at least ten eligible stopping cases. No tuning or new feature map.
Report raw direct maximum, maximum predicted grain, operational uncalibrated
bridge and calibrated bridge under best, last and joint-selected encoders.
Original best-reference bridge predictions remain unchanged. Reusing stopping
labels for selection and calibration is explicit; outer labels are excluded.

Verification and uncertainty
Verify raw source, partitions, checkpoint/preprocessing/source hashes, canonical
IDs, exact truths and counts. Check CPU best replay against existing grain/direct
archives below 1e-4 fitting SD, and mechanical replay below 1e-4 physical truth SD.
Fail closed on identity mismatch or nonfinite mechanical/maximum predictions.
Record all missing auxiliary truth separately; do not alter historical training.
Freeze manifests before inference and require input hashes unchanged afterwards.

Metrics are physical RMSE/MAE/bias, absolute R2, Pearson correlation and prediction
SD/truth SD. Aggregate by arithmetic mean over the two full-cohort repeat metrics.
Paired contrasts use mean squared losses within each physical SVE over repeats;
report signed absolute deltaR2 and deltaRMSE, never percentage change in R2.
Use 4000 paired physical-SVE bootstrap draws, seed 20261117, stratified by size
when pooled. Intervals condition on fixed fitted predictors, are pointwise and
unadjusted, and do not include full selection/retraining uncertainty. Use the same
draws for every candidate/reference/target. Report every size and outcome.
For maximum consequences compare joint-selected and last with the best encoder
using the identical route, so calibration changes are not confused with encoder
changes. No case-specific adoption based on outer results.

Independently recompute metrics, selection and bootstrap results from saved data.
Record provenance and output schema. Preserve all previous studies and manuscript.
No neural optimizer update, additional epoch, target modification or checkpoint
overwrite is permitted. Any further method decision is a separate explicit step.
