MECHANICAL TREE COMPARATORS — 17 November 2026
Specified before any new mechanical tree fit or its outer-evaluation score.

Question: do conventional input-only surrogates recover the mechanical variation
that the matched no-message neural controls predict poorly? Existing graph/control
mechanical point scores motivated this secondary study. No new holdout is claimed.

Cohort and outcomes
Use the existing canonical 940 SVEs and the exact two size-stratified five-fold
partitions. Six targets in their saved order: yield_stress, hardening_modulus,
Wp_lcf, Wp_hcf, sigma_amp_lcf, sigma_amp_hcf. All numerical values are in MPa
(work is stress-strain energy density). Require exact identity/size/grain-count
agreement between the raw249 feature artifact and true_auxiliary_cache.npz.
No mechanical labels are missing or imputed. Each physical SVE is evaluated once
in each repeat. Keep the existing graph and two no-message predictions unchanged.

Predictors and label budgets
Reuse established raw input-only249 descriptors and the fixed RF/HGB settings
from label_matched_trees/protocol.json. These descriptors summarize raw32 grain
inputs (four orientation components, twelve Schmid factors, morphology and degree),
size/count, and six input-only Schmid/morphology interactions. They contain no
CPFEM response, model prediction, maximum or mechanical target. They summarize
texture but are not a full orientation distribution or spatial texture encoding.
Degree is graph-derived; this is a conventional aggregated-input comparator, not
a topology-free feature claim.

Evaluate both fixed budgets, without choosing a winner by outcomes:
  fitting: original inner fitting IDs only (676–678); stopping IDs unused.
  outer_training: fitting plus stopping IDs (751–753).
For each budget use RF, HGB and a size-specific mean of mechanical labels.
All fitted centers, target means and scales use only that budget's IDs. Outer
evaluation IDs are excluded from every fitted preprocessing statistic and model.

RF:128 trees, max_depth8, min_samples_leaf8, max_features0.8, n_jobs1.
RF jointly fits six size-standardized mechanical targets. HGB fits six separate
targets:80 iterations, max_leaf_nodes7, max_depth3, min_samples_leaf30,
learning_rate0.05, l2_regularization1, early_stoppingFalse. Reuse the original
partition initialization_seed. No tuning, additional feature selection, early
stopping, clipping, neural refitting or outcome-dependent model/size choice.

Preprocessing is exactly the existing direct-tree recipe, extended to the six
mechanical targets. Size-specific raw32 grain-weighted feature centers and size9
SVE means are subtracted from the established mean/max/q90/q99/top3 and size blocks.
For each size/target, subtract the fitting mean and divide by the square root of
max(sample variance,1e-6 pooled fitting variance,1e-30); invert those same values.
The size-mean baseline uses the corresponding budget's fitted means.

Outputs and comparisons
Save every prediction in physical units, fit IDs/preprocessing, model pickles,
source hashes, feature schema, fitted model records and runtime/environment.
Report absolute R2, physical RMSE/MAE/bias and Pearson correlation for each repeat,
each of five sizes, and pooled; report arithmetic means across repeats. Also
report prediction/target SD ratios and within-partition ratios to characterize
the controls' compressed variation. Do not infer a causal training failure from
those statistics alone.

For graph versus every fixed reference, report absolute R2 differences and
physical RMSE differences. Average squared losses over the two repeats within
each physical SVE; use4000 paired physical-SVE bootstrap draws, stratified by
size for pooled scope, seed20261117, common draws for all models and targets.
R2 differences use the same resampled truth variance. Positive deltaR2 favors
the graph; negative deltaRMSE favors the graph. RMSE percentage reduction may be
provided as a secondary compatibility statistic, never as percent change in R2.
Intervals are pointwise, conditional on fixed models and the reused development
cohort; no retraining or multiplicity adjustment. All outcomes/scopes are reported.
