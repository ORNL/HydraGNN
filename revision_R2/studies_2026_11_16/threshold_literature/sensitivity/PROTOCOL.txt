Bounded sensitivity to the GPD shape constraint and minimum excess count
Defined 16 November 2026 before computing this sensitivity comparison

Motivation
The completed primary threshold comparison showed frequent lower shape-bound
fits in small SVEs. A requirement of ten excesses also sends high thresholds
to the predicted-maximum fallback for most 125 micrometre SVEs, whereas the
original bridge can use four or more selected grains. These observations
motivate an explanatory sensitivity analysis. They do not justify choosing
a new model from its outer evaluation performance.

Fixed design
Cross lower shape bounds {-0.49,-0.90} with minimum excess counts {10,4}.
The upper shape bound remains0.95. Compare exactly three scalar maps: EQD,
fixed median threshold, and fixed mean-plus-sample-SD threshold. EQD retains
the original candidate quantiles, B=100, m=500, bootstrap definitions, seed
rule and maximum functional. The minimum-count setting applies uniformly to
candidate eligibility and deterministic fallbacks. All other numerical
settings remain identical. The baseline(-0.49,10) arm is recomputed and must
reproduce the existing frozen outputs exactly.

The range below -0.5 is nonregular for standard GPD likelihood asymptotics.
Its finite constrained likelihood can still be optimized here, but no AD
calibration or shape-stability score test is extended into this range. This
study concerns EQD and fixed-threshold aggregation only; it makes no formal
GOF, confidence-coverage or independent-grain claim.

Population and fitting
Use all10,520 saved predicted fields, including7,520 evaluation fields, and
all3,760 observed fields. Preserve every original SVE identity, outer repeat,
fold, case and size. Each new map uses its own field only. Calibrate each arm,
method, fold and case by StandardScaler plus scalar Ridge(alpha10), with a
free intercept and no positivity clipping, on all75 original stopping SVEs.
Do not alter neural checkpoints or use evaluation labels for threshold choice,
calibration fitting, numerical settings or method selection.

Report allfour arms, allthree methods, raw and calibrated predictive accuracy,
and allcase/size cells. Keep observed-field reconstruction separate. Include
selected counts, lower/upper boundary frequencies, numerical failures,
fallbacks and runtime. Import the original calibrated bridge and size-mean
references unchanged. Include paired comparisons using the same shared4000
physical-SVE bootstrap draws from the primary study, averaging repeat squared
errors before resampling. Any simultaneous summary covers the complete
prespecified sensitivity family and does not erase its exploratory origin.

Verification and stopping rule
Preserve original source modules and outputs. Use isolated source-hashed
numerical clones. Verify the expanded negative-shape likelihood against an
independent optimizer before field computation. Check allfield identities,
source hashes, finite predictions and exact baseline-arm reproduction. Finish
this fixed comparison without extending or optimizing the grid after scores.
