Primary endpoint and uncertainty
Compare critical_treatment with bulk_control on raw FULL-N maximum of the NEW
predicted grain peaks, not the original legacy graph head. P is squared error
divided by the ORIGINAL fold's fitting-size/case sample variance. Average both
repeat losses within each physical SVE, then average the twenty size/case cells
equally. Positive risk reduction favors treatment. Report absolute and percentage
reductions with percentile95% intervals from4,000 shared physical-SVE bootstrap
draws stratified by size. Reuse the pinned draws from joint_branch_analysis.
Repeat predictions are not independent specimens and are never averaged into an
ensemble. Intervals are pointwise and conditional on trained models and this
reused940-SVE development cohort, not fresh validation after prior model search.

B is equal-SVE grain mean squared error divided by fitting equal-SVE grain-mixture
population variance. T uses the prescribed fractional true-top-four weights and
the same grain denominator. Report B,T,P and .5B+.5P separately, including all
size/case cells. A favorable B or composite does not certify improved maxima.
Report selected epochs, identity epoch-1 selections, execution lengths and rules.

Grain and critical-grain diagnostics
Score new raw grain peaks and the exact cached original p0 in physical units,
pooled and by size. Ordinary grain metrics pool grain rows; B/T remain equal-SVE
objectives. Paired grain RMSE resamples physical SVEs with all their grains intact.

For each SVE/case, true-critical amplitude diagnostics average squared/absolute
errors and moments over every grain tied at the true full maximum. Near-critical
amplitude uses the same fractional cutoff weights summing to min(4,N) as T.
Thus tied critical labels do not receive arbitrary canonical preference. These
are label-informed magnitude diagnostics, not deployable alternative predictions.

Predicted rankings use descending prediction and canonical grain-row order for
ties. Top1 capture means the predicted winner attains the true maximum. Top4
capture means any of the top min(4,N) predicted rows attains that true maximum.
Record true value at the predicted winner, its fraction of the true maximum and
shortfall; also maximum true value among predicted top4 as a capture fraction.
When the true maximum and all labels are zero, define capture fraction1 and
record the count. Preserve true-max tie counts, grain counts and exact random
top1/top4 chance with those ties. The endpoint remains the actual full-SVE maximum.

For treatment-control and each arm-cached_original, report paired grain RMSE,
critical/near-critical RMSE, and differences in capture diagnostics, all with the
same physical-SVE resamples. These diagnose mechanisms; accurate maximum magnitude
does not require exact localization, and they are not extra adoption gates.
